Valid formats

Size (for `new` or `resize`)

    60x60 (letter `x`)
    60,60 (comma)
    60 60 (whitespace)

Color

    #ffffff
    #ffffffff (alpha in hex)
    #ffffff/100 (alpha in percentage)

    255,255,255 (comma)
    255,255,255,255
    255 255 255 (whitespace)
    255 255 255 255

    $42 (for palette index)
